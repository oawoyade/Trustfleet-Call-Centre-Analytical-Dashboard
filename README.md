# ☎️ TrustFleet Call Centre Analytical Dashboard (2020)

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes call center operations for TrustFleet in the year 2020. The goal is to uncover trends in call volume, customer sentiment, service-level compliance, communication channel usage, and geographic demand. The dashboard offers insights to help stakeholders understand service bottlenecks, sentiment challenges, and regional performance — enabling better workforce planning, training, and operational strategy.

---

## 🗂️ Data Source

The dataset used in this analysis is a synthetic representation of TrustFleet's 2020 customer service records. It includes over 32,000 call interactions, segmented by communication channel, call reason, sentiment classification, SLA compliance, location (state/city), and call center.

---

## 🛠️ Tools and Methodology

- **Microsoft Excel**: Used for data shaping, verification, interactive dashboard creation and quality assurance.
- Employed for computing KPIs such as call distribution, SLA performance, and sentiment scoring.
- **Text Analytics**: Sentiment fields were used to classify interactions into categories like Joy, Sadness, or Neutral.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Structure

The dashboard is organized into four core analytical areas:

---

### Call Volume & Communication Channel Overview
- **Total Calls in 2020**: **32,941**
- **Top Call Reason**: **Billing Questions** (23,462 calls)
- **Most Used Channel**: **Call Center** (10,639 calls), followed by **Chatbot** and **Email**
- **State with Most Calls**: **California** (3,631 calls)

### SLA Performance & Resolution Trends
- **Calls Within SLA**: **4,168**
- **Calls Above SLA**: **20,625**
- **Unresolved Calls**: 8,148 (indicating missed service targets)
- **Most SLA Breaches**: Occurred within **Billing** and **Account Updates**

- **Negative Sentiment**: **11,063 calls**

### Geographic & Call Center Performance
- **Top Cities by Call Volume**:

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 🧍 Customer Behavior & Sentiment
- The majority of calls expressed **negative** or **neutral** sentiment.
- Calls involving **billing** and **account updates** triggered most dissatisfaction.
- **Positive interactions** were typically found in Product Inquiry and General Info calls.

### ⏱️ Service-Level Agreement (SLA) Performance
- Over **62% of calls breached SLA**, pointing to operational inefficiencies.
- SLA compliance needs immediate intervention in **Sales** and **Support** departments.

### 🧭 Regional Demand & Center Load
- **Los Angeles center** is overburdened (42% of total calls), suggesting the need for load redistribution or scaling.
- **Denver center** is underutilized at just 8%.

### 📡 Channel Effectiveness
- **Call Center remains the primary channel**, but chatbots and email show potential for cost-saving automation if optimized.

---

## ✅ Recommendations

1. **Boost SLA Compliance**:
   - Invest in agent training and shift optimization
   - Implement performance-based KPIs and real-time SLA tracking

2. **Reduce Negative Sentiment**
   - Provide customer service training in empathy and resolution techniques
   - Implement early warning systems to flag high-risk customer emotions

3. **Balance Call Center Workload**
   - Redistribute call volume from **Los Angeles** to **Denver**
   - Consider increasing staffing or AI automation in overloaded centers

4. **Enhance Chatbot & Email Efficiency**
   - Optimize chatbots to resolve basic billing and support queries
   - Improve email response workflows and auto-replies to reduce backlog

5. **Prioritize Billing & Account Calls**
   - Redesign billing workflows for faster resolution
   - Offer self-service billing portals to reduce pressure on call center
